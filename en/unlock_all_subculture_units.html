Total War: Warhammer III — Mod

Unity of the Peoples

Every troop of a people for all its factions: unified rosters, shared Regiments of Renown and unlocked faction buildings.

OverviewInstallationHow it worksTroops gainedBuildingsNot affectedLimitsMultiplayer

Overview

In the base game, two factions of the same people do not always get the same troops: Reikland, Gelt's Golden Order and Elspeth's Wissenland each have their own roster, Regiments of Renown are reserved to some factions, and some buildings only exist for a single legendary lord. This mod brings it all together.

Rule: for every people (Empire, High Elves, Norsca…), every faction — player and AI — gets every troop of that people. If a troop can only be recruited from a building reserved to one faction, that building becomes available to the whole people.

Installation

  1. Subscribe to the mod on the Steam Workshop, or copy unlock_all_subculture_units.pack into the game's data folder.
  2. Open the launcher, Mods tab, and tick the mod.
  3. Start a new campaign or load an existing save: the mod is compatible with campaigns in progress.

This mod works with Foreign Troops of Bretonnia, in any load order.

How it works

I. Unified rosters

Each faction gets the troops of the other factions of its people.

II. Shared exclusive units

Regiments of Renown and faction-specific units (Morr units, Imperial Supply…) become available to the whole people.

III. Unlocked buildings

If a troop can only be recruited from a building reserved to one lord, that building becomes available to the whole people.

IV. Units & Spells menu

Every unlocked troop appears in its people's roster.

Not affected

  • Lords and heroes: only troops are unlocked.
  • Rebel, prologue and end-game factions: unchanged.
  • Chaos Dwarf labourer rebels: excluded, so that Greenskins do not get hobgoblins.

Known limits

Units from campaign mechanics: some troops do not come from a building but from a campaign system (Warriors of Chaos pools and Marks, Nurgle daemons, Elspeth's Gardens of Morr, the Huntmarshal's Imperial Supply). They are allowed for every faction, but only appear if the mechanic offers them.
  • Warriors of Chaos: every faction gets the units of every Mark.
  • After a game update that adds units, the mod will need an update to include them.
  • Do not remove the mod from a campaign in progress where you recruited unlocked troops or built unlocked buildings.

Multiplayer

Works in co-op and versus campaigns if every player has the same mod enabled, including when resuming a saved campaign. Made for Immortal Empires.
