Foreign Troops of Bretonnia

Spirits of Athel Loren, foreign orders, the royal hunt, noble beasts, Tilean artillery and fallen knights in the service of the Lady.

Overview

The kingdom of Bretonnia no longer relies only on its knights and peasants. This mod opens its gates to the spirits of Athel Loren, foreign orders, royal huntsmen, noble beasts of the mountains, Tilean artillery and even fallen knights.

16new units
6new buildings
14building levels
7peasant units

Every unit has its own name and description. It keeps the stats, look and abilities of the original unit, but follows the Bretonnian rules: peasant economy, knightly vows, technologies and lord skills. In game, names and texts are in French: the French name is shown below each English name.

Installation

  1. Subscribe to the mod on the Steam Workshop, or copy brt_foreign_units.pack into the game's data folder.
  2. Open the launcher, Mods tab, and tick the mod.
  3. Start a new campaign or load an existing save: the mod is compatible with campaigns in progress.
Units taken from other peoples use their original content (Wood Elves, Vampire Counts, Kislev, Empire): you need to own it.

This mod works with Unity of the Peoples, in any load order.

Buildings

Each building takes a secondary slot, like a barracks or stables, and only one is allowed per province. Foreign troops therefore cost you building slots. Each level requires a minimum settlement level.

Bretonnian elite

Some units are improved versions of Bretonnian units, with reworked stats. They are recruited from new levels added to existing Bretonnian buildings.

Ducal Guard of Bastonne

Elite version of Beastslayers of Bastonne (Foot Squires), tier 3: on par with the best elite infantry of the other peoples (Black Guard, Phoenix Guard), and the most defensive of them, because Bretonnia's elite remains its knighthood.

Peasant economy

Units marked Peasant count towards the faction's peasant limit (2 per region, plus bonuses), exactly like Men-at-Arms or Peasant Bowmen. Above the limit, the usual penalties apply.

Peasant units: Free Company Rabble, Grail Penitents, Royal Hunt Pack, King's Huntsmen, Tilean Siege Mortars, Marienburg Bombards, Ducal Guard of Bastonne.

Knightly vows

The new noble cavalry is bound to the Bretonnian vows, in two ways:

1. Restricted recruitment

Only a lord who has completed the required vow can recruit the unit. A higher vow includes the lower ones (Grail > Questing > Knight's). Select the lord: the units he is not allowed to recruit are locked.

2. Upkeep

Like base-game knights, these units pay +200% upkeep, removed in the army of a lord who completed the matching vow. The vow effect tooltips list the new units.

The Fallen Blood Knights are outside the vow system: any lord can recruit them, with no penalty and no reduction. In exchange, their flat upkeep is high (650, about 36% of the cost): more than Grail Knights under a lord with the Grail Vow (462), but far less than without it (1386).
Only human players are subject to the lock; the AI recruits freely. Units already recruited are never removed.

Integration into the kingdom

  • Bretonnian bonuses: each unit benefits from the technologies and lord skills matching its role (knights, infantry, artillery…).
  • Units & Spells menu: the new units appear in Bretonnia's roster.
  • Custom battles: playable with Bretonnia (without equipment upgrades).
  • AI: the Bretonnian AI builds the new buildings and recruits these units.
  • Immortal Empires: made for the grand campaign.

Multiplayer

Works in co-op and versus campaigns if every player has the same version of the mod and the same content, including when resuming a saved campaign. Ranked and quick battles do not allow mods.

Known limits

  • Building icons, unit cards and 3D models are those of the original peoples.
  • The mod's in-game texts are in French.
  • Do not remove the mod from a campaign in progress that uses its units or buildings: the save might no longer load.
